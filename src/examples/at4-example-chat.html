Some statements in your discipline may count as common knowledge that needs no citation; others clearly need a source. Go through your draft and decide, for each claim, which category it falls into — that judgement is discipline-specific and yours to make. Softening “always” to something defensible (for example “tend to”) would also reduce how much evidence you need to carry.
