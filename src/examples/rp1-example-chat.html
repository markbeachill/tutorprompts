What I am seeing

  • No research question yet. You have a topic and an aim, but nothing that asks something answerable. “To study…” names an area, it does not pose a question.
  • Too broad. The aim packs in BTS, YouTubers, consumer culture and digital media — each could be a dissertation on its own.
  • Overlapping objectives. “Look at campaigns”, “study YouTube” and “explore branding” overlap heavily and use vague verbs (look, talk, explore) rather than researchable actions.

One thing I need from you

Researchability depends on scale. What level is this (e.g. final-year undergraduate, master's) and what is the word count? A 3,000-word essay and a 15,000-word dissertation can support very different scopes, and I do not want to push you to narrow too far or too little.

While you tell me, start shaping a single question. A workable pattern is: How does [one specific thing] affect [one specific outcome] for [one specific group]? Try filling that in with just one campaign or one platform, and paste it back with your level and length.
