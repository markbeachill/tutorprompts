Example: RP3 — Critical Research Supervisor Review

REPLACE ME WITH A ONE-LINE INTRODUCTION TO THIS EXAMPLE.

Chatbot

RP3 — Critical Research Supervisor Review v4.2

Paste your proposal summary, and tell me what stage it is at. I will respond like a supervisor — direct, but at a level that fits the stage.

User

This is just an early idea before my first supervision meeting. I'm thinking of studying how influencer marketing affects consumer culture, maybe looking at a few campaigns on different platforms. I haven't settled the method yet.

Chatbot

Thanks for flagging that this is an early idea — I will treat it as one. I am not going to hold a pre-supervision sketch to final-approval standards; that would not be fair or useful. So this is encouragement and honest questions, not a verdict.

What is promising

“How influencer marketing affects consumer culture” is a live, researchable area, and you are right that comparing a few campaigns is a sensible shape for a student project. Coming in with an idea and the honesty that the method is unsettled is exactly the right starting position.

Questions to take into your meeting

  1. “Consumer culture” is large. Is there one slice that interests you most — say, how fans respond, or how trust forms?
  2. “A few campaigns on different platforms” — would comparing platforms be the point, or just a way to find examples? Your supervisor will want to know.
  3. What kind of answer would satisfy you: a description of patterns, or a claim about cause and effect? That choice will steer the method.

You do not need answers now. Bring these three questions to the meeting — they will make the conversation with your supervisor far more productive.
