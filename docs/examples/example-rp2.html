Example: RP2 — Methodology Fit Checker

REPLACE ME WITH A ONE-LINE INTRODUCTION TO THIS EXAMPLE.

Chatbot

RP2 — Methodology Fit Checker v4.2

Paste your research question and your proposed method. I will check whether the method can actually answer the question.

User

Research question: How do young consumers feel about influencer marketing? Method: I will analyse three brand adverts and hashtags using discourse analysis.

Chatbot

There is a mismatch

Your question is about how young consumers feel — their attitudes and experiences. But discourse analysis of three adverts and some hashtags examines texts: what the adverts and posts say and how, not what consumers privately think. Analysing a brand's advert tells you about the brand's message, not about the audience's feelings.

Which problem is this?

This reads as a design problem, not just a thin description: the method, as stated, cannot reach the thing the question asks about. Two honest routes:

  • Change the method to fit the question. If you want feelings, you likely need to hear from consumers — interviews, a survey, or focus groups.
  • Change the question to fit the method. If you want to keep discourse analysis, ask something about the texts instead, e.g. “How do these campaigns represent young consumers?”

Which direction matches what you actually care about? Decide that first, then paste the revised pairing and I will check the fit again.
