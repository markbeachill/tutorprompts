Example: AT10 — Socratic Tutor

REPLACE ME WITH A ONE-LINE INTRODUCTION TO THIS EXAMPLE.

Chatbot

AT10 — Socratic Tutor v4.2

Give me a piece of your thinking. I will pick one useful starting point and ask you questions, one at a time, to develop it.

User

Choose a useful starting point from this work and ask me Socratic questions about it: BTS fans share branded content online, and this may affect how campaigns spread through social media.

Chatbot

Good starting point: the word “may” in “may affect how campaigns spread”. That's where your thinking is still open.

One question to begin: when fans share branded content, what exactly do you think changes about how a campaign spreads — its reach, its speed, how trustworthy it seems, or something else?

User

I think fan sharing matters because it reaches people without paying. Also BTS are a Japanese band so their campaigns work mainly in Japan.

Chatbot

One quick correction before we go on, because it affects your reasoning: BTS are a South Korean group, not Japanese, and their fanbase is global rather than mainly Japanese. Worth keeping straight, as it changes the “works mainly in one country” idea.

Back to your good point — that fan sharing reaches people without paying. Here is the next question: if reach is free through fans, what does the brand give up in return — for instance, how much control does it keep over the message once fans are the ones spreading it?

User

Like I said, it mainly works because fans share things for free and it reaches people without paying.

Chatbot

Let's pause here for a checkpoint, because we have circled back to the same answer twice.

Where we have got to: you are confident that fan sharing matters mainly because it gives free reach. That is a solid first insight — but we have not yet tested the cost side (control, message drift, or whether free reach is reliable reach).

Three ways forward — your choice:

  • Go deeper on the control trade-off (my last question).
  • Change angle — e.g. how you would actually measure “reaches people”.
  • Stop here if free reach is the single point you wanted to nail down.

Which would you like?
