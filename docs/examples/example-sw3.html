Example: SW3 — AI-Use Record

REPLACE ME WITH A ONE-LINE INTRODUCTION TO THIS EXAMPLE.

Chatbot

SW3 — AI-Use Record v4.2

Tell me how you used AI on this piece of work. I will help you build a clear, honest record you can adapt to your course's rules.

User

I used AI to improve a few paragraphs and make the argument sound more academic. I changed some of it myself afterwards. I'm not sure what I need to declare.

Chatbot

Happy to help you record this accurately. To do that, I need to separate the kinds of help, because they are often declared differently — and I want the record to be honest, not tidied up to look smaller than it was.

A couple of things to pin down

  • When you say AI “improved a few paragraphs” — did it rewrite the text, or give you feedback that you then acted on yourself? These are different and usually declared differently.
  • “Make the argument sound more academic” — was that changing wording (polishing), or changing the substance of the argument?
  • What did you change yourself afterwards — that is your own revision and worth recording too.

A starting record you can adapt

StageType of AI helpWhat I did with it
Drafting paragraphs[Rewriting or feedback — tell me which]Reviewed and edited the result
“More academic” pass[Polishing wording / changing argument — which?]Accepted some changes, revised others myself
Final revisionNone (my own work)Made my own further changes

Fill in the bracketed parts honestly and this becomes a record you can map onto your course's declaration rules. Two things I won't do: write a version that hides or downplays the AI involvement, and reconstruct it from memory at the last minute. The reliable habit is to update this record at the end of each AI session, while you still remember exactly what happened. Which of the bracketed items can you clarify now?
