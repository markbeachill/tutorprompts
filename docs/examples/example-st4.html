Example: ST4 — Reverse Outline Mapper

REPLACE ME WITH A ONE-LINE INTRODUCTION TO THIS EXAMPLE.

Chatbot

ST4 — Reverse Outline Mapper v4.2

Paste your draft, or its paragraphs in order. I will produce a reverse outline — one short line per paragraph describing what it actually does — so you can see the structure you have built. I won't write new text for you.

User

Para 1: Streaming has changed how people listen to music, and this essay looks at its effects on new artists.

Para 2: Spotify pays a small amount per stream. Many small artists earn very little. Some have second jobs.

Para 3: Playlists are powerful. Getting on an editorial playlist can transform an artist's reach overnight.

Para 4: Vinyl sales have risen recently and some fans prefer physical formats.

Para 5: Overall streaming has both helped and hurt new artists.

Chatbot

Reverse outline

ParagraphWhat it doesFunction
1Introduces streaming and states the essay's focus on new artists.Introduction / scope
2Argues that per-stream pay is low, so small artists earn little.Point: income problem
3Argues that playlists can dramatically expand reach.Point: visibility opportunity
4Notes that vinyl sales have risen.Possible digression
5Concludes that streaming both helps and harms new artists.Conclusion

What the map shows

  • Paragraphs 2 and 3 form a clear “harm vs help” pair, which sets up the balanced conclusion in 5 nicely.
  • Paragraph 4 (vinyl) sits outside that pair. Its function is unclear: it is about physical formats and fan preference, not directly about new artists under streaming. As mapped, it reads as a digression.
  • The essay is currently a two-point argument (income down, visibility up) wrapped in an introduction and conclusion.

Decisions for you

Two questions to settle: (1) Does Paragraph 4 earn its place — can you connect vinyl back to new artists' income or reach? If not, it may belong elsewhere or be cut. (2) Are two points enough for your conclusion's “both helped and hurt”, or do you want a third? Decide, then re-map if you move things — I am happy to outline the revised order once you have rearranged it.
