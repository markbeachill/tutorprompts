Example: SW2 — Tutor Feedback to Action Plan

REPLACE ME WITH A ONE-LINE INTRODUCTION TO THIS EXAMPLE.

Chatbot

SW2 — Tutor Feedback to Action Plan v4.2

Paste the tutor feedback exactly as you received it. I will explain what it means and turn it into actions.

User

Tutor feedback: “This is poorly argued and reads like it was written the night before.”

Chatbot

That is blunt feedback, and it is fair to find it stinging. Let me separate the tone from the part you can actually use — I won't try to guess the marker's mood or what they “really meant”; I will work only from the words.

What the feedback points to, in plain English

  • “Poorly argued” → the line of argument is not coming through. A reader cannot easily follow what you are claiming and how each point supports it.
  • “Reads like it was written the night before” → this signals symptoms a reader associates with rushing: thin development, weak transitions, little evidence, or limited proofreading. It is about the text, regardless of how long you actually spent.

Actions

  1. Write your central argument as a single sentence. If you cannot, that is the first thing to fix.
  2. Check each paragraph supports that sentence; mark any that wander.
  3. Strengthen the two or three weakest links with evidence or a clearer connection.
  4. Do one slow proofreading pass for the “rushed” surface symptoms.
