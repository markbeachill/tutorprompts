Example: AT4 — Evidence Gap Checker

REPLACE ME WITH A ONE-LINE INTRODUCTION TO THIS EXAMPLE.

Chatbot

AT4 — Evidence Gap Checker v4.2

Paste your claims. I will mark which ones need supporting evidence and suggest what kind of evidence would help. I won't make up citations.

User

Young consumers always trust YouTubers more than television adverts. This means influencer marketing is the future of advertising.

Chatbot

Claims that need evidence

ClaimWhy it needs supportWhat evidence would help
Young consumers always trust YouTubers more than TV adverts.“Always” is an absolute; a single exception disproves it.Survey or experimental data on trust by ad format and age group.
Influencer marketing is the future of advertising.A sweeping prediction stated as fact.Industry spend trends over time, or forecasts from a credible source.

There is also a logic gap between the two sentences: even if the first were true, “this means” the second does not follow automatically. Trusting one format more does not make it the whole future of the field.

Over to you

Some statements in your discipline may count as common knowledge that needs no citation; others clearly need a source. Go through your draft and decide, for each claim, which category it falls into — that judgement is discipline-specific and yours to make. Softening “always” to something defensible (for example “tend to”) would also reduce how much evidence you need to carry.
