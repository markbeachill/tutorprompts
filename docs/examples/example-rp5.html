Example: RP5 — Guided Topic Brainstorming

REPLACE ME WITH A ONE-LINE INTRODUCTION TO THIS EXAMPLE.

Chatbot

RP5 — Guided Topic Brainstorming v4.2

Tell me the area you are drawn to. I will ask a couple of questions before suggesting anything, so the ideas actually fit you.

User

I want to do a dissertation about beauty ideals and young women on social media.

Chatbot

Good area — rich and well-studied, which helps with sources. Before I suggest topics, two quick questions so the ideas fit your situation:

  1. Are you more interested in the content (how beauty is portrayed) or the effect (how it shapes how young women feel or act)?
  2. What access do you realistically have — can you recruit participants, or would you be working from public posts only?

That said, here are three starting directions to react to:

  1. How a single platform's filters and editing tools shape the beauty norms shown to young women.
  2. How young women themselves talk about and resist beauty ideals in their own posts or comments.
  3. How “body positivity” campaigns by brands are received versus how they are intended.

Which of these pulls at you — and what are your answers to my two questions?

User

I like the second idea best.

Chatbot

Good — the second idea (how young women talk about and resist beauty ideals in their own posts) is appealing because it puts their voices at the centre. Let me run it through a quick viability test in plain terms.

Draft question. Something like: How do young women express resistance to mainstream beauty ideals in their own social-media posts? That is focused and answerable.

Realistic data source. Public posts, captions and comments under a relevant hashtag — accessible without recruiting participants, though you would need to think about ethics and consent even for public material.

Feasible method. A thematic or discourse analysis of a manageable sample (say, a set number of posts) is doable at dissertation scale.

It passes the test. Now the part that has to be yours: write your own one-sentence draft research question for this idea, in your words, and paste it back. I will help you sharpen it from there.
