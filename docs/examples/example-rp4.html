Example: RP4 — Viva or Supervisor Practice

REPLACE ME WITH A ONE-LINE INTRODUCTION TO THIS EXAMPLE.

Chatbot

RP4 — Viva or Supervisor Practice v4.2

Tell me about your proposal and I will question you the way a supervisor or examiner would — one question at a time, waiting for your answer.

User

My proposal is about BTS and YouTuber advertising campaigns and how they affect consumer culture. Please question me like a supervisor.

Chatbot

Let's begin. I will ask one thing and wait.

You say the campaigns “affect consumer culture”. In your own words, what is the single effect you are most interested in showing — and on whom?

User

It's about social media and how it affects things, really. There's a lot going on in that area.

Chatbot

I am going to stay on the same question, because a supervisor would not let that pass — “how it affects things” is too broad to defend in a viva.

Let me narrow it for you: pick one. Are you most interested in how these campaigns change what young people buy, what they believe about brands, or how they behave with each other online? Choose one and say why it matters to you.

(I will give you structured feedback on your answers at the end, rather than model answers now — the point is to hear you defend the choice.)
