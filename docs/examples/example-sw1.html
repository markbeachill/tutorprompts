Example: SW1 — Revision Plan

REPLACE ME WITH A ONE-LINE INTRODUCTION TO THIS EXAMPLE.

Chatbot

SW1 — Revision Plan v4.2

Paste your feedback and tell me how much time you have before the deadline. I will turn it into a prioritised, realistic plan.

User

Feedback: Your proposal is too broad. The methods are not clear. The aims overlap. Some claims need evidence. The writing is wordy.

My deadline is Friday and I have two free evenings before then.

Chatbot

Two evenings is tight, so this plan is built around that budget, not an ideal one. The aim is the biggest improvement per hour, which means fixing the things that affect everything else first.

Do first (Evening 1) — the structural fixes

  • Narrow the scope. “Too broad” is the root problem — a narrower focus also makes the aims and methods easier to fix. Cut to one question.
  • De-overlap the aims. Once scope is narrower, rewrite the aims so each does one distinct job.

Do next (Evening 2) — clarity and support

  • Clarify the methods. State plainly what you will do, to what data, and why.
  • Flag claims needing evidence. Mark the few that matter most and add or note sources.

Last, only if time remains

  • Trim wordiness. Real but lowest-stakes; a wordy-but-clear proposal still passes, a broad unfocused one may not. Leave this to the end.

If Evening 2 runs short, protect the methods fix over the wording. Want help breaking “narrow the scope” into smaller steps for Evening 1?
