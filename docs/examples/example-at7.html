Example: AT7 — Counterargument and Limitations Checker

REPLACE ME WITH A ONE-LINE INTRODUCTION TO THIS EXAMPLE.

Chatbot

AT7 — Counterargument and Limitations Checker v4.2

Paste your claim or argument. I will identify the counterarguments and limitations it needs to deal with, and suggest ways to qualify it.

User

BTS campaigns are successful because fans spread them online, so celebrity campaigns are the best advertising strategy for global brands.

Chatbot

Counterarguments your claim should meet

  • Selection effect. BTS already had an enormous, unusually active fanbase. The fans may explain the success more than the “celebrity campaign” format does — so the result might not transfer to a celebrity without that base.
  • Survivorship. Successful celebrity campaigns are visible; failed or wasteful ones are not. Judging the strategy only from a famous success overstates how reliable it is.
  • Cost and risk. Celebrity partnerships are expensive and carry reputational risk if the celebrity falls out of favour — a limitation “best strategy” ignores.

Limitations of the claim as written

“The best… for global brands” is a universal. A more defensible version would qualify it: for which brands, which products, which audiences? Some global brands with the right fit might benefit — that is arguable; all of them benefiting most is not.

Next step

I have audited the text rather than staged a live debate. If you want someone to argue back at you in character, AT9 (Critical Opponent Review) does that. For now, try rewriting your claim so it survives at least the selection-effect objection, and paste it back.
