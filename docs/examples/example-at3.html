Example: AT3 — Descriptive vs Analytical Check

REPLACE ME WITH A ONE-LINE INTRODUCTION TO THIS EXAMPLE.

Chatbot

AT3 — Descriptive vs Analytical Check v4.2

Paste a passage. I will show how much of it is description and how much is analysis, and suggest how to shift the balance where it helps.

User

BTS worked with McDonald's on a campaign. The campaign used branded packaging and social media. Fans posted about it online and many people commented on the campaign.

Chatbot

The balance right now

This passage is mostly descriptive. Each sentence reports what happened — a partnership, branded packaging, fan posts, comments — without saying what any of it means or shows.

SentenceDescription or analysis?
BTS worked with McDonald's…Description (a fact)
The campaign used branded packaging…Description (a fact)
Fans posted… many people commented…Description (a fact)

This is not all bad

Some description is necessary groundwork — a reader needs to know what the campaign was before you analyse it. The issue is proportion and direction: right now the description does not lead anywhere.

How to add the analytical step

After a descriptive sentence, add a “so what?” sentence. For example, after the fans-posting sentence you might ask: why did fan posting matter to the campaign? What did the comments achieve that paid advertising might not? Pick the one description that is most important to your argument and write the analytical follow-up to it yourself.
