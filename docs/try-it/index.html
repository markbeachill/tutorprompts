Privacy reminder

Use external AI platforms carefully.

These links open external AI platforms. Do not paste private, sensitive or identifiable student work unless you have permission and your institution allows it.

For ordinary extracts of your own work, use the feedback to revise the work yourself and follow your course rules on AI use.
